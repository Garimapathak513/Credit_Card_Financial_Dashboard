📈 Dashboard Highlights

Customer Report
- Weekly & monthly revenue trends (Jan 2023 – Oct 2023)

- Revenue by educational level
- Revenue by income group (High, Med, Low)
- Revenue by age group (20–30, 30–40, 40–50, 50–60, 60+)
- Revenue by gender

 Transaction Report
- Card category performance (Blue, Silver, Gold, Platinum)
- Quarterly revenue & transaction volume (Q1–Q4)
- Revenue by expenditure type (Bills, Entertainment, Fuel, Grocery, Food, Travel)
- Revenue by job type (Businessman, White-collar, Self-employed, Govt, Blue-collar, Retirees)
- Revenue by education type (Graduate, High School, Unknown, Uneducated, Post-Grad, Doctorate)
- Revenue by card type
- Revenue by transaction category (Swipe, Chip, Online)

💡 Business Insights

Top Performers
- **Blue card** dominates revenue: **46M** (vs Silver 6M, Gold 2M, Platinum 1M)
- **Graduate degree** holders generate highest revenue: **22M**
- **Businessmen** lead job types: **17M**
- **Bills** expenditure type: **14M** revenue

Transaction Behavior
- **Swipe transactions** account for majority: **35M** (Chip: 17M, Online: 3M)
- Monthly revenue peaks at **9M** in mid-year
- **Married** individuals contribute highest revenue

Customer Segments
- **High income group** = highest revenue
- **Age group 40–50** = most valuable segment
- Q4 shows strong performance in transaction volume
